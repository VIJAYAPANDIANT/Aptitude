# Quantitative Aptitude: Profit, Loss & Discount

## 1. Concept Definitions & Explanations

- **Cost Price (CP)**: The price at which an article is purchased.
- **Selling Price (SP)**: The price at which an article is sold.
- **Profit (Gain)**: Occurs when Selling Price is greater than Cost Price ($SP > CP$).
- **Loss**: Occurs when Cost Price is greater than Selling Price ($CP > SP$).
- **Marked Price (MP / List Price)**: The price printed/labeled on the article.
- **Discount**: The reduction offered on the Marked Price.
- **Markup**: The increase in price above the Cost Price to set the Marked Price.

---

## 2. Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Basic Calculations
- **Profit** $= SP - CP$
- **Loss** $= CP - SP$
- **Profit %** $= \left( \frac{\text{Profit}}{CP} \times 100 \right)\%$
- **Loss %** $= \left( \frac{\text{Loss}}{CP} \times 100 \right)\%$
- **Discount** $= MP - SP$
- **Discount %** $= \left( \frac{\text{Discount}}{MP} \times 100 \right)\%$

### SP and CP Relations
- **Finding SP**:
  - When there is a Profit: $SP = CP \times \left( \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100} \right)$
  - When there is a Loss: $SP = CP \times \left( \frac{100 - \text{Loss}\%}{100} \right)$
- **Finding CP**:
  - When there is a Profit: $CP = \frac{SP \times 100}{100 + \text{Profit}\%}$
  - When there is a Loss: $CP = \frac{SP \times 100}{100 - \text{Loss}\%}$

### MP to CP Ratio Shortcut (Crucial)
$$\frac{MP}{CP} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100 - \text{Discount}\%}$$

### Successive Discounts
If successive discounts of $d_1\%$ and $d_2\%$ are given, the equivalent single discount is:
$$\text{Net Discount} = \left( d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{100} \right)\%$$

### Dishonest Dealer / Faulty Weights
When a merchant claims to sell at Cost Price but uses a faulty weight:
$$\text{Gain \%} = \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \times 100$$
*Alternatively: $\text{Gain \%} = \left( \frac{\text{True Weight}}{\text{False Weight}} - 1 \right) \times 100$*

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1
An article is sold at a profit of 20%. If it had been sold for Rs. 60 less, there would have been a loss of 10%. Find the Cost Price of the article.

### Problem 2
Find the single discount equivalent to a series of successive discounts of 20%, 10%, and 5%.

### Problem 3
A dishonest shopkeeper sells sugar at cost price but uses a false weight of 900 grams instead of 1 kilogram. What is his overall profit percentage?

### Problem 4
A man buys two watches for a total of Rs. 1600. He sells one at a loss of 10% and the other at a profit of 15%. If he makes no profit or loss on the entire transaction, find the cost price of each watch.

### Problem 5
By selling an article for Rs. 720, a shopkeeper incurs a loss of 10%. At what price should he sell it to gain 20%?

### Problem 6
A retailer marks his goods 40% above the cost price and then allows a discount of 25% on the marked price. Find his net profit or loss percentage.

### Problem 7
A man sells two articles for Rs. 990 each, gaining 10% on one and losing 10% on the other. Find his overall gain or loss percentage on the entire transaction.

### Problem 8
A shopkeeper offers a discount of 10% on the marked price of a laptop and still makes a profit of 20%. If the cost price of the laptop is Rs. 30,000, find its marked price.

### Problem 9
A manufacturer sells a product to a wholesaler at a profit of 10%. The wholesaler sells it to a retailer at a profit of 20%. The retailer sells it to a customer at a profit of 15% for Rs. 15,180. Find the cost price of the product for the manufacturer.

### Problem 10
A man bought some apples at 5 for Rs. 4 and sold them at 4 for Rs. 5. Find his gain or loss percentage.

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Represent the situations using CP**:
   - Let Cost Price be $CP$.
   - Selling Price at 20% profit $= 1.20 \times CP$.
   - Selling Price at 10% loss $= 0.90 \times CP$.
 2. **Set up the equation**:
   - The difference between the two selling prices is Rs. 60.
   $$1.20 \times CP - 0.90 \times CP = 60$$
   $$0.30 \times CP = 60$$
   $$CP = \frac{60}{0.30} = 200$$
3. **Answer**: The Cost Price is **Rs. 200**.

### Solution 2
1. **Combine the first two discounts (20% and 10%)**:
   $$\text{Equivalent Discount } (D_{12}) = 20 + 10 - \frac{20 \times 10}{100} = 30 - 2 = 28\%$$
2. **Combine the result with the third discount (28% and 5%)**:
   $$\text{Equivalent Discount } (D_{123}) = 28 + 5 - \frac{28 \times 5}{100} = 33 - 1.4 = 31.6\%$$
3. **Answer**: The equivalent single discount is **31.6%**.

### Solution 3
1. **Identify the values**:
   - True Value $= 1000$ grams.
   - Faulty Value $= 900$ grams.
   - Error $= 1000 - 900 = 100$ grams.
2. **Apply the Dishonest Dealer Formula**:
   $$\text{Gain \%} = \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \times 100$$
   $$\text{Gain \%} = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = \frac{100}{9}\% = 11.11\%$$
3. **Answer**: His overall profit is **11.11%** (or $11\frac{1}{9}\%$).

### Solution 4
1. **Use variables to represent Cost Prices**:
   - Let the cost price of the first watch be Rs. $X$.
   - The cost price of the second watch is Rs. $(1600 - X)$.
2. **Set up the profit/loss equality**:
   - There is no net profit or loss, so:
     $$\text{Loss on First Watch} = \text{Profit on Second Watch}$$
     $$10\% \text{ of } X = 15\% \text{ of } (1600 - X)$$
     $$0.10X = 0.15(1600 - X)$$
3. **Solve for $X$**:
     $$10X = 15(1600 - X) \implies 2X = 3(1600 - X)$$
     $$2X = 4800 - 3X \implies 5X = 4800 \implies X = 960$$
   - Cost price of the first watch is Rs. 960.
   - Cost price of the second watch is $1600 - 960 = \text{Rs. } 640$.
4. **Answer**: The cost prices are **Rs. 960 and Rs. 640**.

### Solution 5
1. **Find original Cost Price ($CP$)**:
   - Selling price $SP = 720$, loss $= 10\%$.
     $$CP = \frac{SP \times 100}{100 - \text{Loss}\%} = \frac{720 \times 100}{90} = 800$$
2. **Calculate Selling Price for 20% profit**:
     $$New\ SP = CP \times \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100} = 800 \times \frac{120}{100} = 960$$
3. **Answer**: The selling price should be **Rs. 960**.

### Solution 6
1. **Assume a base Cost Price**:
   - Let $CP = 100$.
2. **Find Marked Price ($MP$) and Selling Price ($SP$)**:
   - Marked Price is 40% above CP:
     $$MP = 100 \times 1.40 = 140$$
   - Discount of 25% on MP:
     $$SP = MP \times (1 - 0.25) = 140 \times 0.75 = 105$$
3. **Determine net profit percentage**:
   - Net profit $= SP - CP = 105 - 100 = 5$.
   - Net profit percentage $= \frac{5}{100} \times 100 = 5\%$.
4. **Answer**: His net profit is **5%**.

### Solution 7
1. **Identify the transaction pattern**:
   - Two items are sold at the same selling price (Rs. 990 each).
   - One yields a profit of $X\% = 10\%$ and the other a loss of $X\% = 10\%$.
2. **Apply the standard shortcut**:
   - Under this specific scenario, there is always an overall loss given by:
     $$\text{Overall Loss \%} = \left(\frac{X}{10}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{10}{10}\right)^2 = 1\%$$
3. **Answer**: The merchant incurs an overall **loss of 1%**.

### Solution 8
1. **Use the CP-MP relation formula**:
   $$\frac{MP}{CP} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100 - \text{Discount}\%}$$
2. **Substitute the given values**:
   - $CP = 30,000$.
   - $\text{Profit}\% = 20$, $\text{Discount}\% = 10$.
     $$\frac{MP}{30,000} = \frac{100 + 20}{100 - 10} = \frac{120}{90} = \frac{4}{3}$$
3. **Solve for $MP$**:
     $$MP = 30,000 \times \frac{4}{3} = 40,000$$
4. **Answer**: The marked price is **Rs. 40,000**.

### Solution 9
1. **Understand successive profit multipliers**:
   - Let the cost price for the manufacturer be $CP$.
   - Manufacturer profit to wholesaler $= 10\%$, so selling price is $CP \times 1.10$.
   - Wholesaler profit to retailer $= 20\%$, so selling price is $CP \times 1.10 \times 1.20$.
   - Retailer profit to customer $= 15\%$, so selling price is $CP \times 1.10 \times 1.20 \times 1.15$.
2. **Set up the equation**:
   - Given customer price $= \text{Rs. } 15,180$.
     $$CP \times 1.10 \times 1.20 \times 1.15 = 15,180$$
3. **Solve for CP**:
   - Compute the multiplier: $1.10 \times 1.20 \times 1.15 = 1.32 \times 1.15 = 1.518$.
     $$1.518 \times CP = 15,180 \implies CP = \frac{15,180}{1.518} = 10,000$$
4. **Answer**: The cost price for the manufacturer is **Rs. 10,000**.

### Solution 10
1. **Find cost price and selling price for a common quantity**:
   - Common quantity of apples is LCM of 5 and 4, which is 20 apples.
2. **Calculate Cost Price of 20 apples**:
   - Cost of 5 apples $=$ Rs. 4.
   - Cost of 20 apples $= 4 \times 4 = \text{Rs. } 16$.
3. **Calculate Selling Price of 20 apples**:
   - Selling price of 4 apples $=$ Rs. 5.
   - Selling price of 20 apples $= 5 \times 5 = \text{Rs. } 25$.
4. **Determine profit percentage**:
   - Profit $= 25 - 16 = \text{Rs. } 9$.
   - Profit Percentage $= \frac{9}{16} \times 100 = 56.25\%$.
5. **Answer**: His gain percentage is **56.25%**.
