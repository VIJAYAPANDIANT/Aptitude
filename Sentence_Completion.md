# Verbal Ability: Sentence Completion & Cloze Test

## 1. Concepts & Strategies

Sentence completion and Cloze tests assess your vocabulary, reading comprehension, and knowledge of grammar and collocations.

### Solving Strategies
1. **Identify Sentence Charge**: Determine whether the missing word should be positive, negative, or neutral based on context.
2. **Locate Transition Signals**:
   - **Contrast (Opposition)**: Words like *but, yet, although, despite, however, whereas, conversely* indicate that the blank will have the opposite charge of another part of the sentence.
   - **Support (Agreement)**: Words like *and, furthermore, because, therefore, thus, similarly* indicate that the blank will match the logical flow of the sentence.
3. **Parts of Speech Validation**: Determine if the blank requires a noun, verb, adjective, or adverb.
4. **Eliminate by Collocations**: Check if the words make natural combinations (e.g., we say "commit a crime", not "make a crime").

## 2. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Double Blank)
The manager's __________ behavior towards his team __________ the productivity of the department, leading to low morale.
A) benevolent ... hindered
B) erratic ... undermined
C) diplomatic ... bolstered
D) hostile ... augmented

### Problem 2 (Cloze Test)
**Fill in the blanks (1), (2), and (3) in the passage below:**

> The scientific community is currently grappling with a rise in fraudulent publications. This surge has been partly **(1)** by the intense pressure on academics to publish frequently. To **(2)** this issue, several universities have introduced stricter peer-review processes, hoping to weed out compromised research before it reaches the public. However, critics argue that these measures only address the symptoms rather than the root cause: a deeply **(3)** incentive system.

**Options for Blank (1)**:
A) mitigated
B) exacerbated
C) resolved
D) eliminated

**Options for Blank (2)**:
A) cultivate
B) disregard
C) combat
D) promote

**Options for Blank (3)**:
A) flawless
B) flawed
C) balanced
D) equitable

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Analyze the sentence clues**:
   - The sentence mentions "leading to low morale".
   - This indicates that the overall effect is negative.
   - The second blank represents what the manager's behavior did to "the productivity". Since morale is low, productivity was likely hurt/decreased (negative charge).
   - The first blank describes the behavior leading to this negative result. The behavior was likely negative or inconsistent.
2. **Evaluate the options**:
   - **A) benevolent (positive) ... hindered (negative)**: If behavior was benevolent (kind), it would not hinder productivity. (Mismatch).
   - **B) erratic (unpredictable/negative) ... undermined (weakened/negative)**: Both match the negative tone and flow perfectly.
   - **C) diplomatic (positive) ... bolstered (strengthened/positive)**: Does not align with "low morale".
   - **D) hostile (negative) ... augmented (increased/positive)**: Hostility would not increase productivity. (Mismatch).
3. **Answer**: **B**

### Solution 2
1. **Blank (1)**:
   - Context: "grappling with a rise in fraudulent publications. This surge has been partly [blank 1] by the intense pressure..."
   - The pressure is the cause of the surge (a bad thing). Thus, the pressure *increased* or *worsened* the surge.
   - **exacerbated** means made a problem worse. This fits perfectly.
   - **mitigated** (reduced) and **resolved/eliminated** do not fit.
   - **Answer**: **B (exacerbated)**

2. **Blank (2)**:
   - Context: "To [blank 2] this issue, several universities have introduced stricter peer-review processes..."
   - Universities want to solve/fight/reduce the issue of fraudulent publications.
   - **combat** (fight against) fits this objective.
   - **Answer**: **C (combat)**

3. **Blank (3)**:
   - Context: "address the symptoms rather than the root cause: a deeply [blank 3] incentive system."
   - The system is causing the problems, so the system must be bad or broken.
   - **flawed** (having weaknesses or imperfections) is the only negative adjective that fits.
   - **Answer**: **B (flawed)**
