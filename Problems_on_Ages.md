# Quantitative Aptitude: Problems on Ages

## 1. Concept Definitions & Explanations

- **Present Age**: The current age of a person, typically represented as the base variable $x$.
- **Past Age (n years ago)**: Represented as $x - n$.
- **Future Age (n years hence / from now)**: Represented as $x + n$.
- **Age Difference Invariance**: The difference between the ages of two individuals remains constant throughout their lifetime. For example, if A is 5 years older than B today, A will still be exactly 5 years older than B ten years from now.

---

## 2. Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Linear Formulation
If the present age is $x$:
- Age $n$ years ago $= x - n$
- Age $n$ years from now $= x + n$

### Ratio Applications
If the present ages of $A$ and $B$ are in the ratio $a : b$:
- Let present age of $A = ax$
- Let present age of $B = bx$

### Quick Cross-Multiplication Method for Ratios
If the ratio of ages of $A$ and $B$ is $a : b$ now, and will be $c : d$ after $T$ years:
$$A\text{'s present age} = \frac{a \times T \times (d - c)}{ad - bc}$$
$$B\text{'s present age} = \frac{b \times T \times (d - c)}{ad - bc}$$
*(Note: If the second ratio is in the past, $T$ is negative or absolute depending on equation alignment. Use basic algebra to avoid sign errors).*

---

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1
Ten years ago, the ratio of the ages of a father and his son was $3 : 1$. Ten years hence, the ratio of their ages will be $2 : 1$. Find their present ages.

### Problem 2
The sum of the present ages of a mother and her daughter is 50 years. Five years ago, the mother's age was seven times the daughter's age. What are their present ages?

### Problem 3
A man is 24 years older than his son. In two years, his age will be twice the age of his son. Find the present age of the son.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Set up variables based on past ratio**:
   - Let the ages of father and son 10 years ago be $3x$ and $1x$ respectively.
2. **Determine their present ages**:
   - Father's present age $= 3x + 10$
   - Son's present age $= 1x + 10$
3. **Determine their future ages (10 years hence)**:
   - Father's age 10 years hence $= (3x + 10) + 10 = 3x + 20$
   - Son's age 10 years hence $= (1x + 10) + 10 = x + 20$
4. **Form the equation based on future ratio**:
   - The future ratio is $2 : 1$.
     $$\frac{3x + 20}{x + 20} = \frac{2}{1}$$
     $$3x + 20 = 2(x + 20)$$
     $$3x + 20 = 2x + 40 \implies x = 20$$
5. **Calculate present ages**:
   - Father's present age $= 3(20) + 10 = 70$ years.
   - Son's present age $= 1(20) + 10 = 30$ years.
6. **Answer**: Father is **70 years** old, and Son is **30 years** old.

### Solution 2
1. **Set up the variables**:
   - Let daughter's present age $= d$ years.
   - Mother's present age $= 50 - d$ years.
2. **Form the equation for 5 years ago**:
   - Mother's age 5 years ago $= (50 - d) - 5 = 45 - d$.
   - Daughter's age 5 years ago $= d - 5$.
   - The mother's age was 7 times the daughter's age:
     $$45 - d = 7(d - 5)$$
     $$45 - d = 7d - 35$$
     $$8d = 80 \implies d = 10$$
3. **Calculate present ages**:
   - Daughter's present age $= 10$ years.
   - Mother's present age $= 50 - 10 = 40$ years.
4. **Answer**: Mother is **40 years** old, and Daughter is **10 years** old.

### Solution 3
1. **Set up variables**:
   - Let Son's present age $= s$ years.
   - Father's present age $= s + 24$ years.
2. **Form the equation for 2 years hence**:
   - Son's age in 2 years $= s + 2
   - Father's age in 2 years $= (s + 24) + 2 = s + 26$.
   - In 2 years, Father is twice as old as Son:
     $$s + 26 = 2(s + 2)$$
     $$s + 26 = 2s + 4$$
     $$s = 22$$
3. **Answer**: The son's present age is **22 years**.
