# Data Interpretation: Pie Charts

## 1. Key Conversions & Rules

A **Pie Chart** is a circular graph representing parts of a whole. The total value of a pie chart is always equal to:
- **$100\%$** (if represented in percentages)
- **$360^\circ$** (if represented in degrees)

### Conversion Formulas
1. **Percentage to Degree**:
   $$\text{Degrees} = \text{Percentage} \times \frac{360}{100} = \text{Percentage} \times 3.6$$
2. **Degree to Percentage**:
   $$\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Degrees}}{3.6} = \frac{\text{Degrees}}{360} \times 100$$

### Finding Sector Value
To find the actual numerical value of a sector from the total value ($T$):
- **If sector is in percentage ($P\%$)**:
  $$\text{Value} = \frac{P}{100} \times T$$
- **If sector is in degrees ($D^\circ$)**:
  $$\text{Value} = \frac{D}{360} \times T$$

## 2. Practice Dataset: Monthly Household Budget

A family has a total monthly budget of **Rs. 60,000**. The distribution of expenses is represented by a pie chart with the following sector angles:

- **Rent**: $90^\circ$
- **Food**: $108^\circ$
- **Education**: $72^\circ$
- **Savings**: $54^\circ$
- **Others**: $36^\circ$

*(Check: $90^\circ + 108^\circ + 72^\circ + 54^\circ + 36^\circ = 360^\circ$)*

---

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Degree to Value)
How much money (in Rs.) does the family spend on Food every month?

### Problem 2 (Degree to Percentage)
What percentage of their total monthly budget is saved?

### Problem 3 (Ratio Breakdown)
If the money spent on Education is split between School Fees and Books in the ratio $5 : 3$, how much money is spent on Books?

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Identify parameters**:
   - Total budget $T = \text{Rs. } 60,000$.
   - Food sector angle $D = 108^\circ$.
2. **Calculate the value**:
   $$\text{Food Expense} = \frac{108}{360} \times 60,000$$
   $$\text{Food Expense} = \frac{3}{10} \times 60,000 = 3 \times 6,000 = 18,000$$
3. **Answer**: The family spends **Rs. 18,000** on Food.

### Solution 2
1. **Identify the Savings angle**:
   - Savings $= 54^\circ$.
2. **Convert degrees to percentage**:
   $$\text{Percentage Savings} = \frac{\text{Degrees}}{3.6} = \frac{54}{3.6} = 15\%$$
   - *(Alternative: $\frac{54}{360} \times 100 = \frac{3}{20} \times 100 = 15\%$)*
3. **Answer**: The family saves **$15\%$** of their budget.

### Solution 3
1. **Calculate the total money spent on Education**:
   - Education sector angle $= 72^\circ$.
   $$\text{Education Expense} = \frac{72}{360} \times 60,000 = \frac{1}{5} \times 60,000 = 12,000$$
2. **Apply the ratio division ($5 : 3$)**:
   - The ratio of School Fees to Books is $5 : 3$.
   - Total parts $= 5 + 3 = 8$.
   - Parts allocated to Books $= 3$.
     $$\text{Expense on Books} = \frac{3}{8} \times \text{Education Expense}$$
     $$\text{Expense on Books} = \frac{3}{8} \times 12,000 = 3 \times 1,500 = 4,500$$
3. **Answer**: **Rs. 4,500** is spent on Books.
