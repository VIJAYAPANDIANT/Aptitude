# Verbal Ability: Vocabulary & Analogies

## 1. Word Associations & Relationships

Vocabulary questions evaluate your understanding of word meanings and their relationships.

### Root Word Method (Etymology)
Understanding prefixes, suffixes, and roots helps decode unfamiliar words:
- **Bene** (Good/Well) $\to$ Beneficial, Benevolent, Benediction.
- **Mal** (Bad/Evil) $\to$ Malevolent, Malicious, Malady.
- **Chron** (Time) $\to$ Chronological, Chronic, Anachronism.
- **Loqu/Locut** (To speak) $\to$ Loquacious, Eloquent, Colloquial.

### Analogies
Analogy questions present a pair of words and ask you to find another pair with the same relationship:
- **Synonym/Antonym Relationship**: *Giant : Dwarf :: Peak : Valley*
- **Part to Whole**: *Page : Book :: Leaf : Tree*
- **Tool to Worker**: *Scalpel : Surgeon :: Gavel : Judge*
- **Cause and Effect**: *Virus : Influenza :: Spark : Fire*
- **Degree of Intensity**: *Warm : Hot :: Cool : Cold*

## 2. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Synonym)
Choose the word closest in meaning to the capitalized word:
**CAPRICIOUS**
A) Steadfast
B) Whimsical
C) Courageous
D) Melancholic

### Problem 2 (Antonym)
Choose the word opposite in meaning to the capitalized word:
**EPHEMERAL**
A) Transient
B) Eternal
C) Ethereal
D) Superficial

### Problem 3 (Analogy)
Choose the pair that best expresses a relationship similar to that of the original pair:
**LIGHTHOUSE : SHIPS**
A) Runway : Airplanes
B) Map : Travelers
C) Siren : Ambulance
D) Traffic Light : Pedestrians

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Define the target word**:
   - **Capricious** means given to sudden and unaccountable changes of mood or behavior; unpredictable or impulsive.
2. **Evaluate the options**:
   - **Steadfast** means firm and unwavering (Antonym).
   - **Whimsical** means playfully quaint or fanciful, especially in an appealing and amusing way; acting on impulse (Synonym).
   - **Courageous** means brave.
   - **Melancholic** means sad and pensive.
3. **Answer**: **B**

### Solution 2
1. **Define the target word**:
   - **Ephemeral** means lasting for a very short time; fleeting or transient.
2. **Evaluate the options**:
   - **Transient** means lasting only for a short time (Synonym).
   - **Eternal** means lasting or existing forever; without end (Antonym).
   - **Ethereal** means extremely delicate and light in a way that seems too perfect for this world.
   - **Superficial** means existing or occurring at or on the surface.
3. **Answer**: **B**

### Solution 3
1. **Analyze the relationship in the original pair**:
   - **Lighthouse : Ships**
   - A lighthouse guides and directs ships safely to avoid hazards.
2. **Evaluate the options**:
   - **Runway : Airplanes** $\to$ Airplanes land on a runway (Place of action).
   - **Map : Travelers** $\to$ A map guides and directs travelers to navigate and reach their destination safely. This mirrors the guiding relationship.
   - **Siren : Ambulance** $\to$ Siren is a tool on an ambulance.
   - **Traffic Light : Pedestrians** $\to$ Traffic lights direct both cars and pedestrians, but it is primarily regulatory rather than navigating a journey.
3. **Answer**: **B**
