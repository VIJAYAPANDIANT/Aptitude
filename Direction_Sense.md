# Logical Reasoning: Direction Sense

## 1. Direction Map & Angles

To solve direction-based questions, visualize the 4 main cardinal directions and 4 sub-directions:

```
               NORTH (N)
                   |
       NW          |          NE
         \         |         /
           \       |       /
WEST (W) ------------+------------ EAST (E)
           /       |       \
         /         |         \
       SW          |          SE
                   |
               SOUTH (S)
```

### Turn Rules
- **Right Turn**: $90^\circ$ turn in Clockwise direction.
- **Left Turn**: $90^\circ$ turn in Counter-Clockwise direction.

---

## 2. Key Concepts & Formulas

### Shortest Distance (Pythagoras Theorem)
If a person travels $a$ meters in one direction and then makes a $90^\circ$ turn and travels $b$ meters, the shortest distance (straight line) from the starting point is:
$$\text{Shortest Distance} = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$

### Shadow Concepts
- **Case 1: Sunrise (Morning)**
  - The sun is in the **East**.
  - All shadows are cast towards the **West**.
  - If you face **North**, your shadow falls to your **Left**.
  - If you face **South**, your shadow falls to your **Right**.

- **Case 2: Sunset (Evening)**
  - The sun is in the **West**.
  - All shadows are cast towards the **East**.
  - If you face **North**, your shadow falls to your **Right**.
  - If you face **South**, your shadow falls to your **Left**.

- **Case 3: Noon (12:00 PM)**
  - The sun is directly overhead.
  - No shadows are cast.

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Distance & Direction)
A man walks 10 km North, turns Right and walks 6 km, then turns Left and walks 5 km, and finally turns Right and walks 2 km.
1. In which direction is he now with respect to his starting point?
2. What is the shortest distance between his starting point and final position?

### Problem 2 (Shadow)
One morning after sunrise, Suresh was standing facing a pole. The shadow of the pole fell exactly to his right. Which direction was Suresh facing?

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Trace the path (assuming starting point is $(0,0)$)**:
   - Walk 10 km North: Position $= (0, 10)$
   - Turn Right (East) and walk 6 km: Position $= (6, 10)$
   - Turn Left (North) and walk 5 km: Position $= (6, 15)$
   - Turn Right (East) and walk 2 km: Position $= (8, 15)$
2. **Find direction with respect to starting point**:
   - The final position $(8, 15)$ has both positive x (East) and positive y (North).
   - Hence, he is in the **North-East** direction.
3. **Calculate shortest distance**:
   - Total horizontal displacement $x = 6 + 2 = 8$ km.
   - Total vertical displacement $y = 10 + 5 = 15$ km.
   - Shortest Distance:
     $$d = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17 \text{ km}$$
4. **Answer**: He is in the **North-East** direction, at a shortest distance of **17 km**.

### Solution 2
1. **Analyze the morning condition**:
   - It is morning, so the Sun is in the **East**.
   - Therefore, all shadows fall towards the **West**.
   - The shadow of the pole falls to Suresh's **Right**.
2. **Determine Suresh's orientation**:
   - Since the shadow falls to Suresh's right, his Right side must be pointing towards the **West**.
   - If West is to his Right, then his Left is to the East, his back is to the North, and he is facing **South**.
3. **Answer**: Suresh was facing **South**.
