# Logical Reasoning: Blood Relations

## 1. Family Tree Mapping Symbols

To solve blood relation puzzles efficiently, represent the relationships visually using a standard set of symbols:

- **Male**: Represented by a Square ($\square$) or plus sign ($+$).
- **Female**: Represented by a Circle ($\bigcirc$) or minus sign ($-$).
- **Married Couple**: Connected by double horizontal lines ($=$).
- **Siblings**: Connected by a single horizontal line ($-$).
- **Generation Gap**: Connected by a vertical line ($|$), with the older generation on top and the younger generation below.

### Key Vocabulary Definitions
- **Maternal**: Relationships from the mother's side (e.g., Maternal Uncle = mother's brother).
- **Paternal**: Relationships from the father's side (e.g., Paternal Aunt = father's sister).
- **Sibling**: Brother or sister.
- **Spouse**: Husband or wife.
- **Niece**: Daughter of one's brother or sister.
- **Nephew**: Son of one's brother or sister.
- **Cousin**: Child of one's uncle or aunt (Note: "Cousin brother" or "Cousin sister" is grammatically incorrect; use only "Cousin").

---

## 2. Types of Problems & Strategies

### Type 1: Pointing / Narrative Problems
A person points to a photograph or describes a person's relation.
- **Strategy**: Start solving from the last relation mentioned (usually preceded by "my" or "of") and work backwards.

### Type 2: Coded Relations
Symbols represent relations, e.g., $A + B$ means "A is the mother of B".
- **Strategy**: Break down the expression term-by-term and draw a mini family tree. Check genders and generation gaps to eliminate wrong choices quickly.

---

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Narrative)
Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to that boy?

### Problem 2 (Coded Relations)
If:
- $P \times Q$ means "P is the brother of Q"
- $P + Q$ means "P is the father of Q"
- $P - Q$ means "P is the sister of Q"
Which of the following expressions means "A is the uncle of C"?
1. $A + B - C$
2. $A \times B + C$
3. $A - B \times C$
4. $A \times B - C$

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Analyze Suresh's statement from the back**:
   - "my mother" $\to$ Suresh's mother.
   - "the only son of my mother" $\to$ The only son of Suresh's mother must be Suresh himself.
   - "He is the son of [the only son of my mother]" $\to$ He is the son of Suresh.
2. **Determine the relation**:
   - The boy is Suresh's son.
   - Therefore, Suresh is the **Father** of the boy.
3. **Answer**: Suresh is the **Father** of the boy in the photograph.

### Solution 2
1. **Understand what "A is the uncle of C" means**:
   - $A$ must be Male (since he is an uncle).
   - $A$ must be in the generation above $C$ (specifically, brother of $C$'s father or mother).
2. **Evaluate the options**:
   - **Option 1**: $A + B - C$
     - $A + B \implies A$ is the father of $B$.
     - $B - C \implies B$ is the sister of $C$.
     - This means $A$ is the father of $C$. (Incorrect).
   - **Option 2**: $A \times B + C$
     - $A \times B \implies A$ is the brother of $B$ (Male $\checkmark$).
     - $B + C \implies B$ is the father of $C$.
     - Since $A$ is the brother of $C$'s father ($B$), $A$ is the uncle of $C$. (Correct).
   - **Option 3**: $A - B \times C$
     - $A - B \implies A$ is the sister of $B$ (Female $\to$ Aunt. Incorrect).
3. **Answer**: Option **2** ($A \times B + C$) is the correct expression.
