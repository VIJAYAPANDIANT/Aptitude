# Data Interpretation: Bar & Line Graphs

## 1. Key Calculations in Data Interpretation

Data Interpretation (DI) sets frequently rely on three fundamental mathematical calculations:

### 1. Percentage Growth / Decrease (Percentage Change)
To find the percentage change from an initial value to a final value:
$$\text{Percentage Change} = \frac{\text{Final Value} - \text{Initial Value}}{\text{Initial Value}} \times 100$$
- A positive result indicates **Growth / Increase**.
- A negative result indicates **Decline / Decrease**.

### 2. Average (Arithmetic Mean)
$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of Values}}{\text{Number of Periods/Items}}$$

### 3. Ratio Comparison
To find the ratio of Value A to Value B, express them as a simplified fraction: $\frac{A}{B}$ or $A : B$.

## 3. Practice Problems

### Problem 1 (Percentage Growth)
Find the percentage growth in the Sales of ABC Corporation from 2022 to 2025.

### Problem 2 (Average Calculation)
Calculate the average Net Profit of the company over the five-year period from 2021 to 2025.

### Problem 3 (Ratio & Profit Margin)
In which year was the **Profit Margin** (defined as $\frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100$) the highest?

### Problem 4 (Percentage Growth)
Find the percentage decline in Net Profits of ABC Corporation from 2023 to 2024.

### Problem 5 (Ratio Analysis)
What is the ratio of the total Net Profits of the years 2021 and 2022 combined, to the total Sales of the years 2024 and 2025 combined?

### Problem 6 (Comparative Difference)
What is the difference between the average Sales and the average Net Profits of ABC Corporation over the five-year period (2021 to 2025)?

### Problem 7 (Sales Target)
If Sales in 2026 are expected to grow by $25\%$ over the Sales in 2025, what will be the expected Sales (in Rs. Crores) in 2026?

### Problem 8 (Profit-to-Sales Comparison)
For how many years was the Net Profit more than $13\%$ of the Sales in that year?

### Problem 9 (Profit Growth Comparison)
By what percentage did the Net Profit grow in 2025 compared to the Net Profit in 2024?

### Problem 10 (Sales-to-Profit Comparison)
What was the total Sales of all the years in which the Net Profit was less than 20 Rs. Crores?

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. **Identify the values**:
   - Sales in 2022 (Initial) $= 150$ Rs. Crores.
   - Sales in 2025 (Final) $= 200$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Apply the percentage change formula**:
   $$\text{Percentage Growth} = \frac{200 - 150}{150} \times 100 = \frac{50}{150} \times 100 = \frac{1}{3} \times 100 = 33.33\%$$
3. **Answer**: The percentage growth in Sales is **$33.33\%$**.

### Solution 2
1. **Sum the Net Profits**:
   - Total Profits $= 15 + 18 + 25 + 20 + 30 = 108$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Divide by the number of years**:
   - Number of years $n = 5$.
   $$\text{Average Net Profit} = \frac{108}{5} = 21.6 \text{ Rs. Crores}$$
3. **Answer**: The average Net Profit is **$21.6$ Rs. Crores**.

### Solution 3
1. **Calculate the Profit Margin for each year**:
   - **2021**:
     $$\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{15}{120} \times 100 = 12.5\%$$
   - **2022**:
     $$\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{18}{150} \times 100 = 12\%$$
   - **2023**:
     $$\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{25}{180} \times 100 \approx 13.89\%$$
   - **2024**:
     $$\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{20}{160} \times 100 = 12.5\%$$
   - **2025**:
     $$\text{Profit Margin} = \frac{30}{200} \times 100 = 15\%$$
2. **Compare the margins**:
   - The margins are $12.5\%, 12\%, 13.89\%, 12.5\%, 15\%$.
   - The highest value is $15\%$, which occurred in 2025.
3. **Answer**: The profit margin was highest in **2025**.

### Solution 4
1. **Identify the values**:
   - Net Profit in 2023 (Initial) $= 25$ Rs. Crores.
   - Net Profit in 2024 (Final) $= 20$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Apply the percentage decrease formula**:
   $$\text{Percentage Decline} = \frac{25 - 20}{25} \times 100 = \frac{5}{25} \times 100 = 20\%$$
3. **Answer**: The percentage decline in Net Profit from 2023 to 2024 is **$20\%$**.

### Solution 5
1. **Calculate the required sums**:
   - Total Net Profits (2021 + 2022) $= 15 + 18 = 33$ Rs. Crores.
   - Total Sales (2024 + 2025) $= 160 + 200 = 360$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Express as a simplified ratio**:
   $$\text{Ratio} = \frac{33}{360} = \frac{11}{120}$$
   - Therefore, the ratio is $11 : 120$.
3. **Answer**: The ratio is **$11 : 120$**.

### Solution 6
1. **Calculate the average Sales**:
   - Total Sales $= 120 + 150 + 180 + 160 + 200 = 810$ Rs. Crores.
   - Average Sales $= \frac{810}{5} = 162$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Identify the average Net Profit**:
   - Average Net Profit $= 21.6$ Rs. Crores (from Solution 2).
3. **Calculate the difference**:
   $$\text{Difference} = 162 - 21.6 = 140.4 \text{ Rs. Crores}$$
4. **Answer**: The difference is **$140.4$ Rs. Crores**.

### Solution 7
1. **Identify Sales in 2025**:
   - Sales in 2025 $= 200$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Apply the growth percentage ($25\%$)**:
   $$\text{Expected Sales (2026)} = 200 \times \left(1 + \frac{25}{100}\right) = 200 \times 1.25 = 250 \text{ Rs. Crores}$$
3. **Answer**: The expected Sales in 2026 will be **$250$ Rs. Crores**.

### Solution 8
1. **Refer to the profit margins calculated in Solution 3**:
   - **2021**: $12.5\%$ (Not more than $13\%$)
   - **2022**: $12\%$ (Not more than $13\%$)
   - **2023**: $13.89\%$ (More than $13\%$)
   - **2024**: $12.5\%$ (Not more than $13\%$)
   - **2025**: $15\%$ (More than $13\%$)
2. **Count the qualifying years**:
   - Years with profit margin $> 13\%$ are 2023 and 2025 (total 2 years).
3. **Answer**: There were **2** years.

### Solution 9
1. **Identify the values**:
   - Net Profit in 2024 (Initial) $= 20$ Rs. Crores.
   - Net Profit in 2025 (Final) $= 30$ Rs. Crores.
2. **Apply the percentage growth formula**:
   $$\text{Percentage Growth} = \frac{30 - 20}{20} \times 100 = \frac{10}{20} \times 100 = 50\%$$
3. **Answer**: The Net Profit grew by **$50\%$**.

### Solution 10
1. **Identify the years where Net Profit was less than 20 Rs. Crores**:
   - 2021: Net Profit $= 15$ Rs. Crores (Less than 20)
   - 2022: Net Profit $= 18$ Rs. Crores (Less than 20)
   - 2023: Net Profit $= 25$ Rs. Crores (Not less than 20)
   - 2024: Net Profit $= 20$ Rs. Crores (Not less than 20)
   - 2025: Net Profit $= 30$ Rs. Crores (Not less than 20)
2. **Sum the Sales of these qualifying years**:
   - Sales in 2021 $= 120$ Rs. Crores
   - Sales in 2022 $= 150$ Rs. Crores
   - Total Sales $= 120 + 150 = 270$ Rs. Crores
3. **Answer**: The total Sales was **$270$ Rs. Crores**.
